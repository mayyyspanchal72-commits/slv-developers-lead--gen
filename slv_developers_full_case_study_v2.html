01 · Project overview
Scope & commercial agreement

Project scope

SLV Developers needed a robust performance marketing engine to generate high-quality real estate leads. The goal was to drive targeted traffic and capture buyer intent through optimized Meta Lead Generation campaigns — not just impressions, but high-probability prospects for the sales team to close.

Full-funnel Meta Ads management

Commercial agreement

Engaged as the sole performance marketing partner with end-to-end ownership: campaign creation, audience targeting, creative direction, and budget optimization. Remunerated on a performance-based model — 20% commission on total ad spend managed, aligning incentives with the client's ROI.

Performance-based · 20% commission
02 · Client requirements
What the client needed
High-intent lead generation
Primary objective was generating qualified leads to drive site visits and property sales — not vanity metrics. Every rupee had to translate into a real prospect the sales team could call.
Precise audience targeting
Reach prospective homebuyers, working professionals (25–55), and real estate investors actively looking for properties in Bengaluru's IT corridors — not a broad, spray-and-pray approach.
Cost efficiency without quality sacrifice
Maintain a highly competitive Cost Per Lead (CPL) while keeping inquiry quality high. Low CPL is meaningless if the sales team wastes time on junk leads — both sides of the equation matter.
Lead quality control
Filter out "junk" leads through form design, audience qualification, and creative messaging that self-selects serious buyers — ensuring the sales team's calling time is spent on high-probability prospects.
Transparency & ROI reporting
Clear weekly and monthly reporting on ad spends, impressions, CPL, and lead volumes — required to justify the performance-based 20% commission structure and give the client full visibility.
Complete media plan executed: Full campaign architecture, budget allocation, ad set structure, and targeting parameters documented in the master media plan. View complete media plan
03 · Strategy & execution
3-phase campaign architecture
Phase 1

Pixel & data infrastructure

  • Meta Pixel deployed across slvdevelopers.in
  • Page View event — traffic & drop-off tracking
  • Lead event — form submit optimization signal
  • Purchase event — token advance / site booking
  • Offline conversion sync to close the ROAS loop
Phase 2

Audience architecture

  • Core: Age 25–55, IT corridor micro-markets
  • Retargeting: Page View ≠ Lead → urgency creatives
  • 1–3% Lookalike from Purchase + Lead events
  • Exclusion lists to avoid repeat-contact waste
  • Layered interest + behavioural signals
Phase 3

Creative & budget scaling

  • Carousel ads — floor plans, amenities, location
  • Video ads — drone footage, site walkthroughs
  • 70–80% budget → lowest-CPL ad sets
  • 20–30% → Pixel-based retargeting
  • Continuous A/B testing, winner scaling

08 · Business outcome
Value delivered to SLV Developers

Predictable lead pipeline

Converted a fixed monthly budget into a reliable, scalable revenue pipeline. The sales team received a consistent flow of 100–580 warm leads per month, directly supporting year-end closing targets.

Full-funnel transparency

Pixel tracking from first Page View to Purchase/Booking event gave the client clear ROAS visibility — every rupee traced from ad impression to site visit to token advance.

Industry-beating CPL

Average CPL of ₹189.57 against a real estate industry benchmark of ₹350+. In December, optimization pushed CPL to ₹104.96 — over 3× better than industry average.

Quality-first approach

Lead form design, audience qualification layers, and creative self-selection filters ensured junk leads were minimized. The client's sales team worked a high-probability prospect list, not a cold-call dump.
