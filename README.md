## 📋 Client Requirements

These were the five non-negotiable requirements defined at project kickoff. Every campaign decision was made against this brief.

### 1. 🎯 High-Intent Lead Generation
> *"Generate qualified leads to drive site visits and property sales — not vanity metrics."*

The client needed leads that actually convert — people who fill a form because they're genuinely interested in buying, not accidental clicks. Every optimization decision prioritized lead quality over raw volume.

### 2. 🗺️ Precise Audience Targeting
> *"Reach prospective homebuyers, working professionals, and real estate investors in and around Bangalore."*

The target persona: **Age 25–55**, working in Bengaluru's IT corridors (Whitefield, Electronic City, Sarjapur Road), with intent signals around property search, home loans, and investment. Not a broad spray-and-pray — a surgical approach.

### 3. 📉 Cost Efficiency Without Quality Sacrifice
> *"Maintain a highly competitive CPL without sacrificing inquiry quality."*

Low CPL is meaningless if the leads are junk. The challenge was to keep CPL below industry benchmarks (₹350+ for real estate) while ensuring the sales team could actually convert the inquiries.

### 4. 🔍 Lead Quality Control
> *"Filter out junk leads so the sales team spends time on high-probability prospects."*

Achieved through:
- Lead form design with qualifying questions
- Audience layering to exclude low-intent segments
- Creative messaging that self-selects serious buyers (price points, location specifics, project details upfront)

### 5. 📊 Transparency & ROI Reporting
> *"Clear weekly and monthly reporting on ad spends, impressions, and lead volumes to justify the 20% commission."*

Weekly performance summaries + monthly deep-dives covering CPL trends, creative fatigue signals, audience saturation, and budget recommendations — all shared transparently with the client.

## ⚙️ Strategy & Execution

The campaign was built in three structured phases:

### Phase 1 — Pixel & Data Infrastructure

Before spending a single rupee on ads, the data foundation was laid:

```
Meta Pixel Deployment → slvdevelopers.in
│
├── Page View Event       → Track all traffic & measure landing page drop-offs
├── Lead Event            → Fire on form submission → optimize algorithm for converters
└── Purchase Event        → Token advance / site booking → closes the ROAS loop
     └── Offline Conversion Sync → Match sales team records back to Meta
```

**Why this matters:** Without accurate Pixel events, Meta's algorithm optimizes blindly. With them, it learns the exact profile of a person who submits a lead form — and finds more of them.

### Phase 2 — Audience Architecture

Three-layer audience strategy:

| Layer | Audience Type | Strategy |
|-------|--------------|----------|
| **Top of Funnel** | Core Demographics | Age 25–55, IT corridor interests, real estate intent signals, Bengaluru geo |
| **Middle of Funnel** | Retargeting | Page View ≠ Lead → Re-engage with urgency creatives (limited units, price offers) |
| **Prospecting** | Lookalike Audiences | 1–3% LAL seeded from Pixel-verified Lead + Purchase events |

**Exclusion lists** were applied at every layer to prevent overlap and avoid re-targeting people who had already submitted a lead.

### Phase 3 — Creative Strategy & Budget Scaling

| Format | Purpose | Content |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Carousel Ads | Trust-building | Floor plans, amenities, location maps, pricing |
| Video Ads | Engagement & recall | Drone footage, site walkthroughs, testimonials |
| Single Image | Direct response | Urgency offers, call-to-action, key USPs |

**Budget allocation rule:**
- **70–80%** → Lowest-CPL, proven ad sets (winner scaling)
- **20–30%** → Pixel-based retargeting campaigns
- **Sunset rule:** Any ad set exceeding 2× the target CPL for 3+ consecutive days was paused and budget reallocated

**Bid Strategy:** `ABSOLUTE_OCPM` (Optimized Cost Per Mille) — letting Meta's algorithm optimize delivery toward lead events while controlling cost per outcome.

### 🔍 Key Creative Insights

**⭐ Star Creative — AD-2 on SLV_Leadform_Bengalore_30/55**
- 653 leads at ₹98.40 CPL — the single highest-volume, lowest-cost creative in the entire campaign
- CTR of 2.91% is 3× higher than the next best active ad (AD-4 at 0.95%)
- Drove the December peak almost single-handedly
- Ran on ABSOLUTE_OCPM bidding with ₹2,150/day budget — proves that higher budget + right creative = algorithmic efficiency, not just volume

**📌 Budget Consolidation Decision**
When AD-2 on Bengalore_30/55 showed consistent sub-₹150 CPL in early December, budget was shifted from the ₹600/day and ₹900/day ad sets into the ₹2,150/day set. This is the core optimization move that created the December peak — a textbook example of winner-scaling.

**⚠️ Sunset Decision — Ad-3**
Ad-3 generated only 1 lead at ₹2,105 CPL. Despite having 22,383 impressions, the creative failed to convert. It was identified as a testing creative and was not scaled. Clear evidence of disciplined budget management — not every ad works, and the job is to identify losers fast.

## 🧠 Key Learnings

These are the performance marketing insights this project validated in the real world:

1. **Pixel maturation takes 3–4 months in real estate** — September and October's higher CPLs were expected. December's ₹104 CPL was only possible because of the Pixel data accumulated in those early months. Don't kill campaigns before they learn.

2. **Winner-scaling beats budget spreading** — Consolidating budget into the single best-performing ad set (AD-2, Bengalore_30/55) at ₹2,150/day was more effective than running 4 ad sets at ₹500/day each. Concentrated budget + good creative = algorithmic efficiency.

3. **CTR is a leading indicator of CPL** — AD-2's 2.91% CTR correlated directly with its ₹98 CPL. Ads with under 1% CTR consistently had CPLs above ₹220. Monitor CTR weekly as an early warning system.

4. **Frequency management is critical in a limited geo** — Bengaluru is a large city, but targeting a specific buyer profile (IT professional, 25–55, property intent) creates a finite audience. After December's peak, January saw rising frequency and higher CPL — a signal that fresh creatives were needed.

5. **Lead form design is half the optimization** — The Bengalore_30/55 ad set consistently outperformed Leadform_30/50 (despite similar audiences) suggesting that form design and question flow materially affects conversion rate.
