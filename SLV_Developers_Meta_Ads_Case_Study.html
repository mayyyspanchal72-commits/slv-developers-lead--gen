Strategy & Execution

Three structured phases — pixel infrastructure, layered audiences, disciplined creative scaling.

Phase 1

Pixel & Data Infrastructure

Before a single rupee was spent, the Meta Pixel was deployed on slvdevelopers.in so the algorithm could learn — not guess.

  • Page View → tracks traffic & landing-page drop-off
  • Lead → fires on form submit, optimises for converters
  • Purchase → token advance / booking, closes the ROAS loop
  • Offline sync → matches sales records back to Meta
Phase 2

Audience Architecture

A three-layer targeting model, with exclusions applied at every layer to prevent overlap.

  • Top of funnel — age 25–55, IT-corridor interest, Bengaluru geo
  • Mid funnel — retarget Page View ≠ Lead with urgency creative
  • Prospecting — 1–3% lookalikes seeded from verified Leads
Phase 3

Creative & Budget Scaling

Carousel, video and single-image formats rotated by funnel stage, on ABSOLUTE_OCPM bidding.

70–80% winner ad sets 20–30% retargeting

Sunset rule: any ad set at 2× target CPL for 3+ days was paused and reallocated.

Key Learnings

What this project validated about real-estate performance marketing.

01

Pixel maturation takes 3–4 months in real estate

September and October's higher CPLs were expected — December's ₹104.96 was only possible because of the Pixel data accumulated earlier. Don't kill campaigns before they learn.

02

Winner-scaling beats budget spreading

Consolidating budget into the single best ad set (AD-2 at ₹2,150/day) outperformed running four smaller sets in parallel.

03

CTR is a leading indicator of CPL

AD-2's 2.91% CTR correlated directly with its ₹98 CPL; ads under 1% CTR consistently ran above ₹220. Monitor CTR weekly as an early warning system.

04

Frequency management is critical in a limited geo

A finite buyer profile in a large city still saturates — January's rising frequency after December's peak signalled it was time for fresh creative.

05

Lead form design is half the optimisation

Bengalore_30/55 consistently outperformed Leadform_30/50 on similar audiences — form design and question flow materially affect conversion.
